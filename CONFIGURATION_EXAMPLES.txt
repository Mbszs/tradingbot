When optimizing in Strategy Tester:

Priority 1 (Optimize First):
  ✅ Confidence Threshold (0.60 - 0.80, step 0.05)
  ✅ ATR Min/Max Multipliers
  ✅ Session times (broker specific)

Priority 2 (Fine-tune):
  ✅ Swing Lookback (15-30)
  ✅ Min OB Body Size (30-100)
  ✅ Volume Multiplier (0.8-1.3)

Priority 3 (Risk Preference):
  ✅ Risk %
  ✅ TP Risk:Reward
  ✅ Max trades per session

Do NOT Optimize:
  ❌ SL Buffer (keep 5-15)
  ❌ OTE Fib levels (standard ICT)
  ❌ Max spread (broker dependent)

Optimization Method:
1. Use genetic algorithm
2. Minimum 1 year data
3. Test on out-of-sample period
4. Forward test on demo ≥1 month

Problem: No trades opening
Solution 1 - Lower confidence threshold to 0.65
Solution 2 - Increase ATR max to 2.5
Solution 3 - Check session times alignment

Problem: Too many losing trades
Solution 1 - Increase confidence threshold to 0.75
Solution 2 - Tighten ATR range (0.7-1.8)
Solution 3 - Increase min OB body size to 70
Solution 4 - Reduce max trades per session to 1

Problem: Circuit breaker triggering frequently
Solution 1 - Reduce risk % to 0.5%
Solution 2 - Increase confidence threshold
Solution 3 - Increase max daily DD to 4%
Solution 4 - Review losing trades in backtest

Problem: Spread errors
Solution 1 - Increase max spread points to 2500
Solution 2 - Trade only during liquid hours
Solution 3 - Switch to ECN broker
Solution 4 - Avoid news events

Problem: Positions closing too early
Solution 1 - Disable break-even move
Solution 2 - Increase TP RR to 2.0
Solution 3 - Don't use partial TP

📊 SESSION TIME CALCULATOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Formula: EA_Hour = GMT_Hour + Server_Offset

Examples:
  If your broker is GMT+2:
    Asia Start:  23 + 2 = 1 (next day)
    Asia End:    6 + 2 = 8
    London Start: 7 + 2 = 9
    London End:  11 + 2 = 13

  If your broker is GMT-5:
    Asia Start:  23 - 5 = 18 (same day)
    Asia End:    6 - 5 = 1 (next day)
    London Start: 7 - 5 = 2
    London End:  11 - 5 = 6

  If your broker is GMT+0:
    Use default: 23, 6, 7, 11
