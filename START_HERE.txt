The EA trades based on ASIAN and LONDON SESSIONS.

These sessions are defined in GMT time:
   - Asia: 23:00-06:00 GMT
   - London: 07:00-11:00 GMT

But YOUR broker's data is in THEIR timezone (e.g., GMT+2).

If you use GMT times with GMT+2 data, the EA trades at the
WRONG HOURS → loses money because it's not catching the real
session dynamics!

Example:
   ❌ Wrong: EA set to 23:00 on GMT+2 data = trading at 1 AM GMT
   ✅ Right: EA set to 1:00 on GMT+2 data = trading at 23:00 GMT
