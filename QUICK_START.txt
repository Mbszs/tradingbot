💡 TRADING TIPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Best Trading Days:
   ✅ Monday-Thursday
   ✅ Avoid major news events (NFP, FOMC, CPI)
   ✅ Highest activity during London session

2. Session Characteristics:
   🌏 Asia: Range-bound, defines liquidity
   🇬🇧 London: High activity, sweeps Asia range
   
3. What EA Looks For:
   1️⃣ Previous session high/low marked
   2️⃣ Price sweeps beyond level (wick)
   3️⃣ Price closes back inside (rejection)
   4️⃣ Market structure aligns (MSS)
   5️⃣ Price retraces to zone (OB/FVG/OTE)
   6️⃣ Confidence score ≥ 0.7
   7️⃣ Entry executed

4. Manual Override:
   - You can manually close trades anytime
   - EA will respect your closures
   - Max trades per session still enforced
