================================================================================
                         ALL NEW PROTECTIONS
================================================================================

1. ✅ VOLATILITY SPIKE FILTER (CRITICAL)
   - Compares current ATR to 20-period average
   - Blocks trades when ATR > 1.5x normal
   - Prevents trading during news explosions
   → Protects against: NFP, FOMC, war news, economic crises

2. ✅ SPREAD PROTECTION (CRITICAL)
   - Monitors spread in real-time
   - Blocks trades if spread > 30 points
   - Prevents entry during wide spread spikes
   → Protects against: News-related spread widening

3. ✅ DAILY LOSS LIMIT (NEW)
   - Tracks daily equity from start of day
   - Stops trading if daily loss > 2%
   - Resets at midnight
   → Protects against: Catastrophic daily losses, revenge trading

4. ✅ MAX TRADES PER DAY (NEW)
   - Limits trades to 3 per day maximum
   - Resets at midnight
   → Protects against: Overtrading, emotional trading

5. ✅ NEWS TIME AVOIDANCE (NEW)
   - Avoids trading ±1-2hrs around major news:
     * NFP: First Friday 12:30 GMT
     * CPI: 2nd Tuesday 12:30 GMT
     * FOMC: Wednesday 18:00 GMT
     * European/US session starts
   → Protects against: Scheduled high-impact events

6. ✅ ULTRA-LOW RISK (NEW)
   - Risk reduced to 0.1% per trade (was 0.3%)
   - $10,000 account = $10 risk per trade
   → Protects against: Large losses

7. ✅ EQUITY-BASED POSITION SIZING (NEW)
   - Automatically reduces position size during drawdown
   - 5% drawdown = 5% smaller positions
   - Never goes below 50% of normal size
   → Protects against: Compounding losses, helps recovery

8. ✅ CIRCUIT BREAKER RE-ENABLED (TIGHTER)
   - Stops all trading at 5% total drawdown (was 8%)
   - Last line of defense
   → Protects against: Account blow-up
