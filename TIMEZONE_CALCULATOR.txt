broker's timezone.

📍 STEP 1: FIND YOUR BROKER'S GMT OFFSET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Method A: Check Market Watch
   1. Open MT5
   2. Look at Market Watch window (Ctrl+M)
   3. Check current time displayed
   4. Compare to actual GMT time (google "current GMT time")
   5. Calculate difference

   Example:
   Market Watch shows: 15:30
   Actual GMT time: 13:30
   Difference: +2 hours → Your broker is GMT+2

Method B: Check Your Broker's Website
   - IC Markets: GMT+2 (summer) / GMT+3 (winter)
   - Pepperstone: GMT+2 (summer) / GMT+3 (winter)
   - OANDA: GMT-5
   - FXCM: GMT-5
   - Exness: GMT+0
   - XM: GMT+2 (summer) / GMT+3 (winter)

Method C: Look at Strategy Tester Data
   1. Run a quick backtest (any EA)
   2. Check first candle time on M5 chart
   3. If it's a Monday, should start around midnight
   4. Compare to GMT Monday midnight
   5. Calculate offset

🧮 STEP 2: CALCULATE YOUR SESSION TIMES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Formula: EA_Setting = GMT_Time + Your_Offset

Standard Session Times (GMT):
   Asia: 23:00 - 06:00
   London: 07:00 - 11:00

Run Visual Mode Backtest:
   1. Strategy Tester → Visual Mode ON
   2. Run 1 week backtest
   3. Watch chart

You should see:
   ✅ "SESSION STARTED: Asia" in Journal around Asian hours
   ✅ "SESSION STARTED: London" in Journal around London hours
   ✅ Session lines appearing on chart
   ✅ Trades only during these sessions

If trades happen at random times → Timezone still wrong!

⚠️ DST (DAYLIGHT SAVING TIME) WARNING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Some brokers change offset with DST:
   - Summer: GMT+2
   - Winter: GMT+3

If your backtest spans summer AND winter, you may need to:
   Option 1: Test each period separately with correct offset
   Option 2: Use average offset (GMT+2)
   Option 3: Accept slight misalignment in winter months
